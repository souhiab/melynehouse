# Store-owner confirmation checklist

These items cannot be safely resolved from the public storefront alone.

1. **Commerce activation:** the staging rebuild now has PostgreSQL catalog management, server-side price/availability validation, recoverable archival, and COD order records. Before live use, the owner must provision the production database/storage, approve the fulfillment workflow, connect customer notifications and any payment/delivery services, and perform staging acceptance. Checkout remains visibly marked as a prototype.
2. **Refund versus exchange wording:** the public exchange policy says monetary refunds are not accepted, while the public payment/security page mentions a 30-day refund policy. Confirm the legally current wording and replace the contradiction in both source pages.
3. **Contact numbers:** the public footer, contact page, complaint/exchange page, and product WhatsApp links expose different number formats/numbers. Confirm the single canonical sales/support number and where each number should appear.
4. **Product descriptions:** 31 of 34 public products have no description. Confirm descriptions, materials, included pieces, care instructions, and measurements before adding any such claims.
5. **Delivery and payment promises:** confirm which public statements remain current, including cash on delivery, card/PayPal references, free-delivery labels, and any 24–48 hour wording. The prototype does not convert a design-image promise into store policy.
6. **Variant inventory:** public variant quantities were captured on 2026-09-26 and can change. A production store must use live server-side availability at add-to-cart and checkout time.
7. **Legal/SEO review:** have the store owner or counsel approve final policy copy, business identity details, metadata, and structured-data claims before deployment.
